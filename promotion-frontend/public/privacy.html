Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information the MatchConnect app and website ("MatchConnect", "we", "us") collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. MatchConnect is operated by Vignesh, based in Tamil Nadu, India.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and your login credentials.
  • Team and match details: team name, players you add, match challenges, tournament registrations and match scores you enter.
  • Bookings: ground, umpire and tournament booking details (dates, slots, venue, amounts).
  • Messages: messages you send to other captains inside the app to coordinate a match.
  • Feedback: anything you send us through the feedback form or by email.

Information collected automatically or with your permission

  • Location: if you use the "Near Me" feature and allow location access, we use your location to show grounds close to you. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • Device notification token: a token that lets us send you push notifications (for example, a challenge accepted or a booking confirmed).
  • Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address, device/browser type and request time, used for security and troubleshooting.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only the payment status, amount and transaction ID needed to confirm your booking.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in.
  • To connect teams, book grounds and umpires, run tournaments and show live scores.
  • To send booking confirmations, notifications and service emails.
  • To process payments, refunds and resolve disputes.
  • To keep the platform safe, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To improve the app based on feedback.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other users: some profile information, such as your name and team details, is visible to other users so that matchmaking, bookings and tournaments work.
  • Service providers that help us run MatchConnect, listed below.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Advertising

MatchConnect does not currently show third-party ads. If we add ads in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

5. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data as described on our Delete Account page, except for records we must keep by law (for example, payment and transaction records).

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login credentials confidential.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile details in the app.
  • You can turn off location and notifications in your device settings.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time. See how to delete your account.

8. Children

MatchConnect is meant for users who are at least 18 years old, or who have permission from a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without that permission. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MatchConnect grows. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Continued use of MatchConnect after an update means you accept the updated policy.
