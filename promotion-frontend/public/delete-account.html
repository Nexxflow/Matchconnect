Delete Your MatchConnect Account

Last updated: 21 September 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to matchconnectmuon@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MatchConnect account".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your MatchConnect account, and include the phone number you registered with, so we can verify that the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, phone number and login credentials).
  • Your team memberships, match challenges and messages linked to your account.
  • Your device notification token.

What we may keep

  • Payment and transaction records (such as booking amounts and Razorpay transaction IDs), which we must keep for accounting and legal reasons.
  • Records needed to resolve an open dispute or refund, kept only until the matter is closed.
  • Match and tournament results that other users also depend on may be kept in anonymised form (without your name).

Need help?

Read our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data, or write to matchconnectmuon@gmail.com.
